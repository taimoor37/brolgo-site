How to spot what to automate in your business

By Taimoor Ahmed · 4 min read

Most businesses we talk to know they should be using AI and automation. Few know where to start. The tools are not the hard part. Picking the right job for them is.

Here is the simple test we use with clients.

Look for the copy and paste

Ask your team one question: what do you copy from one place and paste into another? Enquiries into a CRM. Invoices into accounting software. Form answers into a spreadsheet. Every one of those is a likely automation.

Score each task on three things

  1. How often it happens. Daily beats monthly.
  2. How much time it takes. Add it up across the whole team for a month.
  3. How rule based it is. If you could write the steps on a sticky note, it's a good fit.

Tasks that score high on all three are your first projects. Tasks that need a lot of judgement can still be helped by AI, but start with the simple wins.

Fix the process before you automate it

Automating a messy process just makes the mess faster. Map how the work actually flows today, who does what and where it gets stuck. Often you'll find steps you can simply remove.

Start small and measure

Pick one workflow, automate it, and measure the hours saved over a month. A good first project pays for itself quickly and gives your team confidence in the next one.

Where AI fits in

Classic automation moves data between systems. AI adds the ability to read and write: summarising emails, drafting documents, pulling details out of PDFs or turning a meeting recording into a workflow. Combined, they take whole chunks of admin off your team's plate.
