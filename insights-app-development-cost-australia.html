How much does it cost to build an app in Australia in 2026?

By Taimoor Ahmed · 8 min read

In 2026, building an app in Australia typically costs $20,000 to $80,000 for a simple app, $50,000 to $200,000 for a medium app, and $150,000 to $400,000 or more for a complex one, before GST. Where you land depends on features, integrations, compliance needs and who builds it. Below are the real ranges, city by city rates for Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne, and the ways we see founders keep costs under control.

App development cost in Australia: 2026 price ranges

These are typical quotes from Australian developers and agencies in 2026, excluding GST:

The ranges are wide because "an app" can mean anything from a simple internal tool to a full SaaS platform. The fastest way to narrow your number is to write down exactly what each type of user needs to do.

Developer rates in Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne

Most of an app's cost is people's time, so hourly rates matter. Published 2026 surveys put typical agency rates at roughly:

  • Sydney: about $120 to $250 an hour, the highest in the country.
  • Melbourne: about $100 to $220 an hour.
  • Brisbane: about $100 to $170 an hour, noticeably lower at the top end.

Freelancers usually charge less, from around $50 an hour for juniors to $200 for senior specialists. Boutique agencies tend to sit around $100 to $150 an hour, and larger agencies $150 to $250. Many teams now pair a local project lead with a remote engineering team, which can bring the overall cost down a lot without dropping quality, as long as there's one clear person accountable for your project.

What drives the cost of building an app

  1. Number of features. Every screen, user role and workflow adds design, build and testing time.
  2. Integrations. Connecting to Xero, Salesforce, payment gateways or government systems often takes longer than the app itself.
  3. Compliance. Health, NDIS and finance apps need audit trails, careful data handling and more testing.
  4. Web, mobile or both. A web app that works well on phones is usually much cheaper than separate iPhone and Android apps.
  5. How clear the requirements are. Vague scope is the number one reason projects blow out. A few days of planning up front saves weeks later.

Ongoing costs most people forget

Building the app is only the start. Budget for:

  • Maintenance: a common rule of thumb is 15 to 20 percent of the build cost each year for updates, security fixes and small improvements.
  • Hosting: anywhere from $50 to a few hundred dollars a month, depending on users and setup.
  • App store fees if you publish to Apple or Google, plus paid services like SMS, maps, AI or payments.
  • GST on top of quotes, if your developer is registered.

How AI tools are changing app development costs

Tools like Lovable, Bolt and Replit now let founders build a working first version themselves for the cost of a monthly subscription. That's a big shift. Instead of paying an agency to build everything from scratch, many businesses now build a prototype, test it with real users, then pay a team to make it production ready.

That last step matters. AI built apps often have gaps in security, data access and hosting that don't show up in a demo. Fixing them is usually a fraction of the cost of a full build, but skipping it can be very expensive later. Our pre launch checklist covers the most common gaps, and if your app is already growing, read signs your app has outgrown Lovable.

How to keep your app development costs down

  • Start with the smallest version that solves one real problem, then grow it.
  • Write down what each type of user needs to do before anyone writes code.
  • Choose a web app first unless you truly need phone features like offline use or the camera.
  • Compare quotes on what's included, not just the total. Our guide to choosing a software developer lists what to check.
  • Make sure the code, hosting and data are in your name, so you can change developers without starting again.

Common questions

How much does a simple app cost in Australia?

A simple app with a handful of screens and one type of user typically costs $20,000 to $80,000 in 2026, before GST. If you've already built a first version with an AI tool like Lovable or Bolt, getting it production ready can cost far less than a full build.

Is it cheaper to build an app in Brisbane than in Sydney or Melbourne?

Usually, yes. Brisbane agency rates sit at roughly $100 to $170 an hour, compared with up to $220 in Melbourne and $250 in Sydney. The bigger saving often comes from how the team is set up, for example a local lead with a dedicated remote engineering team.

How much does it cost to maintain an app each year?

Plan for around 15 to 20 percent of the original build cost each year, plus hosting and any paid services. That covers security updates, bug fixes and small improvements as you learn from users.

Can I save money by building my app with AI tools?

Yes, for the first version. AI tools are a cheap way to prove people want your app. Before real customers and real data arrive, have it reviewed for security, permissions, backups and hosting, because those gaps are common in AI built apps.
