About Brolgo

A small team that gets software over the line.

Brolgo is a Brisbane based custom software business. We take apps and MVPs to production and help them scale, working with Australian startups and businesses.

Why we started

Building the first version got easy. Finishing it didn't.

AI tools now let anyone build a working app in a weekend. We kept meeting founders and business owners who had done exactly that, then got stuck. The app worked, but it wasn't secure, it broke under real use, or nobody knew how to host it properly.

Brolgo exists to close that gap. We bring years of experience in product management, business analysis and software delivery across SaaS, CRM and HR tech, and we use it to turn promising prototypes and manual processes into software businesses can rely on.

Our name

Why Brolgo?

The brolga is a tall Australian crane and Queensland's bird emblem. It's known for its dance, where pairs move in step together.

That's how we like to work with clients: in step, from first idea to launch. The red dot in our logo is the brolga's red head.
