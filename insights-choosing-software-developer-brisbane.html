How to choose a software developer in Brisbane

By Taimoor Ahmed · 6 min read

Choosing who builds your software is one of the biggest decisions you'll make for your product. Get it right and you have a partner who helps you grow. Get it wrong and you can lose months and a lot of money before you even launch.

Whether you're looking at a freelancer, a small studio or a larger agency in Brisbane, here's what to check before you sign anything.

1. Look for people who ask about your business first

A good developer wants to understand who your users are, what problem you're solving and what success looks like before they talk about technology. If the first meeting is all about frameworks and hourly rates, that's a warning sign. Software built without a clear understanding of the business usually needs rebuilding later.

2. Ask to see similar work

Ask for examples close to what you need: a client portal, a SaaS product, an app that handles payments or sensitive data. Names may be confidential, but a good team can still walk you through what they built, the problems they hit and how it's going now.

3. Understand how they'll work with you

Ask how often you'll see progress. The best teams work in short cycles of one or two weeks, with a demo at the end of each one. That way you can spot problems early and change direction without wasting a whole budget. Be wary of anyone who disappears for three months and comes back with a finished product.

4. Get clear on who does the work

Many Brisbane agencies use a mix of local and remote developers, and that's fine. What matters is that you have one clear point of contact who understands your business, speaks plainly, and is accountable for the result. Ask who you'll talk to week to week, and who actually writes the code.

5. Make sure you own everything

This one is non negotiable. The code, the hosting accounts, the domain and the data should all be in your name. You should be able to change developers at any time without starting over. Get it written into the agreement.

6. Compare quotes properly

The cheapest quote often leaves things out: testing, security, hosting setup, documentation or support after launch. The most expensive isn't always the best either. Ask each developer what's included, what isn't, and how they handle changes in scope. Our guide to app development costs in Australia gives you realistic ranges to compare against.

7. Ask what happens after launch

Software needs ongoing care: security updates, bug fixes and improvements as you learn from users. Ask whether they offer support after launch, how quickly they respond to problems, and what it costs.

Red flags to watch for

  • A fixed price quote for a big project before they really understand it.
  • No written scope, or vague promises like "we'll build whatever you need".
  • Reluctance to put code and accounts in your name.
  • No testing mentioned at all.
  • Pressure to sign quickly.

A good first step

You don't have to commit to a full build to find out if a developer is right for you. A small, paid first piece of work, like a review or a scoping session, shows you how they think and communicate before the big decision.

Common questions

How much do software developers charge in Brisbane?

Brisbane agencies typically charge around $100 to $170 an hour in 2026, a little below Sydney and Melbourne. Our app development cost guide has full project ranges.

Should I hire a freelancer or a software agency?

A freelancer can suit a small, well defined job. An agency is usually safer for a product you'll keep growing, because you get testing, cover when someone is away and one point of accountability.

What should I ask a software developer before hiring?

Ask how they'll understand your business, how often you'll see progress, who actually writes the code, whether you'll own the code and accounts, and what happens after launch.
