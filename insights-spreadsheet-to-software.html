Spreadsheet to software: when it's time to build a proper tool

By Taimoor Ahmed · 4 min read

Almost every business we work with started on spreadsheets. That's a good thing. Spreadsheets are fast, flexible and free. But at some point the spreadsheet stops helping and starts holding you back.

Five signs you've outgrown it

  • Only one person really understands it, and everyone worries about what happens when they're on leave.
  • There are several versions floating around by email and nobody is sure which is current.
  • Mistakes are getting expensive, like wrong prices, missed bookings or double handling.
  • You need to prove things, such as who changed what and when, for compliance or audits.
  • Customers or staff need access but you can't safely give them the whole file.

Check off the shelf first

Before building anything, look at existing tools for your industry. If one fits 80% of how you work, it's usually the better choice. Custom software makes sense when your process is what makes you different, or when you'd otherwise be stitching five tools together.

Build small, then grow

You don't need to replace everything at once. Start with the part that hurts most, often a simple portal or internal tool, and keep the spreadsheet for the rest. With AI assisted development, a focused first version can be ready in weeks, not months.

Keep ownership

Whoever builds it, make sure the code, hosting and data are in your name. You should be free to change developers without starting again.
