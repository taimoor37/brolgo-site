Custom software · Disability services

A compliance ready platform for disability support workers

An NDIS provider was running its mentoring and support services on spreadsheets, paper and memory. Support workers lost time to admin, and proving compliance at audit time was hard work.

Client NDIS service provider, Australia

Product designWeb appAI assisted buildHelp centre and support

What we did

  • Built a platform their support workers now use every day, working side by side with the client and using AI assisted development to move fast
  • Made onboarding configurable, so each new provider can set it up to match their own processes
  • Put audit trails and compliance features first, given how tightly NDIS services are regulated
  • Set up a help centre, a customer support portal and bug reporting, with alerts going straight to the team

The result

Less time on admin for support workers, a much stronger compliance and governance position, and a product the client now offers to other NDIS providers.

Data and automation · Retail

Retail analytics platform and insights reporting

A retail customer experience consultancy needed a reliable way to receive customer data from national retailers and turn it into regular insight reports for retail leaders.

Client Retail customer experience consultancy, Australia

AWSSecure file transferAnalytics platformRetail insights

What we did

  • Set up a secure file transfer server in the cloud so national retail chains can send customer data files safely
  • Built an analytics platform that pulls the retailer data together and powers all of their reporting
  • Built a weekly flow that moves incoming data into their customer feedback platform
  • Produce monthly and quarterly insight reports, including a national consumer channel report for a large retail buying group

The result

Retailers share data securely on a set schedule, and reports come straight from one analytics platform. Leadership gets clear, regular insights without anyone chasing files or rebuilding spreadsheets.

AI automation · Operating model

From a one hour meeting to a full operating workflow

A care and support services organisation wanted to redesign how new clients move through the business, from first handover to funding, staffing and scheduling, with Salesforce at the centre.

Client Care and support services organisation, Australia

Process mappingSalesforceAI meeting analysisSOPs

What we did

  • Turned a recorded leadership workshop into a first draft operating workflow using AI, in a fraction of the usual time
  • Mapped every function in swimlanes: onboarding, funding, workforce planning, recruitment and scheduling
  • Defined the Salesforce triggers and data needed to hand work between teams automatically
  • Set up a step by step register with clear ownership, ready to become SOPs and a responsibility matrix

The result

Leadership had a clear, visual first draft ready for their executive team within days. Fewer handoffs, clear ownership and a plan for what to automate next.
